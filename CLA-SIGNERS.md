# Contributors who have agreed to CLA.md

One line per external contributor signature, added by pull request. Fields: account (hosting service and account identifier) · agreement version · `cla:sha256:` of the exact `CLA.md` text agreed to (recompute with `shasum -a 256 CLA.md`) · date · `countersigned-by:` (a maintainer's account).

The completed signature form, with legal names and signatures, is retained privately by r5e Inc; nothing personal appears here. Contributions made directly by r5e Inc are owned by r5e Inc and do not require an entry below. A new CLA version requires a new line.
