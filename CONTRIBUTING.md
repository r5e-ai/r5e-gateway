# Contributing to r5e-gateway

`r5e-gateway` is licensed under `AGPL-3.0-only`. r5e Inc also offers it under commercial terms; that is what the contributor agreement makes possible. External contributions are accepted under two things:

- the Developer Certificate of Origin — sign every commit with `git commit -s`; and
- the r5e Contributor License Agreement in [`CLA.md`](CLA.md) — agreed once. You keep your copyright; the agreement lets r5e Inc distribute your contribution under AGPL-3.0 and under other terms. Read it, send the completed signature form privately to <thomas@r5e.io>, and open a pull request adding a line to [`CLA-SIGNERS.md`](CLA-SIGNERS.md) (account, version, `CLA.md` SHA-256, date). A maintainer countersigns. Nothing personal goes into public history.

Every new source file you add should carry `SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-only` on its first comment line.

Changes land with tests or vectors that pin the behaviour they introduce. Issues, questions, and forks are welcome without any of the above.
